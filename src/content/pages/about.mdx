Crow Flies Distance Calculator is a small, independent site that measures the distance between places. It started with a single straight line calculator and is growing into a set of tools for radius maps, travel distances, halfway points and flight distance.

Every tool runs in your browser, needs no account and is free to use.

## How the distances are calculated

Straight line distances are worked out with Vincenty's formulae on the WGS 84 ellipsoid, the same model of the Earth that GPS uses. That method is accurate to within half a millimeter on the model, so the limit on accuracy is how precisely the two places are pinned down, not the maths.

Driving, cycling and walking distances come from routes calculated on OpenStreetMap road and path data. Travel times assume free-flowing traffic and no stops.

## Where the data comes from

- **Maps:** OpenStreetMap contributors, with map tiles from OpenFreeMap.
- **Place and address search:** Photon, a search engine built on OpenStreetMap data.
- **Routes:** the routing server run by FOSSGIS on OpenStreetMap data.
- **City list:** GeoNames.
- **Coastlines and country borders:** Natural Earth.

If you spot a mistake in the map itself, you can [report it to OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/fixthemap) so it gets fixed for everyone.

## How the content is checked

Every distance, heading and travel time quoted in an article is produced by the same code that runs the calculators, not typed in by hand. Facts that come from outside, such as official mileage rates, are checked against the organization that publishes them, and the date of the last update is shown on each page.
