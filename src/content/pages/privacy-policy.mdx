This policy explains what information is handled when you use crowfliesdistancecalculator.com ("the site", "we", "us"). The short version: the site has no accounts, no sign-up and no forms that collect your details, and distances are calculated in your own browser.

## What we do not collect

We do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to use the tools. We do not sell personal information.

## What stays in your browser

- **Distance calculations** run on your device. The places you choose are not stored by us.
- **Your location** is only used if you choose "Use my location" and your browser asks for, and receives, your permission. It is used on your device to place the starting point.
- **Your unit choice** (miles, kilometers or nautical miles) is saved in your browser's local storage so the site remembers it next time. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
- **Shared links** contain the two places you measured, in the web address itself. Anyone you send the link to can see those places.

## Services that receive requests

To show maps, find addresses and plan routes, your browser contacts the services below directly. Like any website, each one receives your IP address and the request it needs to answer.

| Service | What it receives | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Photon (komoot) | The text you type into a place box | To suggest matching places and addresses |
| OpenFreeMap | The map area you are viewing | To draw the street map |
| FOSSGIS routing server | The coordinates of your two points | To calculate driving, cycling and walking routes |
| Cloudflare | Standard request information | To host and deliver the site and protect it from abuse |

These services handle data under their own privacy policies.

## Cookies and advertising

The site itself does not set cookies to track you. We may show advertising to keep the tools free. If we do, ads are provided by Google and its partners.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
- You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting [Google's Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com/). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at [aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

Where the law requires it, you will be asked for consent before advertising cookies are used, and you can change your choice at any time.

To learn more, see [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

## Children

The site is a general-audience tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## Your choices

You can use every tool without sharing your location, block or delete cookies and local storage in your browser, and opt out of personalized ads using the links above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date below.
