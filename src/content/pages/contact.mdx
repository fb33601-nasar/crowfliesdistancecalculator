The quickest way to reach us is by email:

<p class="contact-email"><a href="mailto:contact@crowfliesdistancecalculator.com">contact@crowfliesdistancecalculator.com</a></p>

We read every message and usually reply within two working days.

## Reporting a wrong result

To help us find the problem quickly, include:

- the page you were using,
- the two places or addresses you entered,
- the result you saw, and the result you expected,
- the link from the **Copy link** button, if you can.

If the issue is a missing road, a wrong address or a misplaced building on the map, it is usually in the underlying map data. You can [report it to OpenStreetMap](https://www.openstreetmap.org/fixthemap) directly, which fixes it for every map that uses the data.

## Suggesting a tool

Tell us what you were trying to measure and what you would expect the tool to show. Most of the tools on this site started as a question someone could not answer with an ordinary map.
